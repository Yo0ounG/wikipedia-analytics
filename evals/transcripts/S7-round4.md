English Language Learning App: Research Recommendations

ANALYSIS SUMMARY
I analyzed Wikipedia interest in the English language across four language editions over 24 months (September 2024 – August 2026) using official Wikimedia pageview data. All four languages show declining attention to the English language topic.

KEY FINDINGS
- Polish: -18.3% decline (325 → 266 daily views; largest audience)
- Turkish: -16.5% decline (279 → 233 daily views; most resilient)
- Czech: -22.8% decline (121 → 93 daily views; shows seasonal pattern: peaks Feb, low Jul)
- Ukrainian: -35.6% decline (340 → 219 daily views; steepest decline at -3.8%/month)

RESEARCH PRIORITY RANKING (for audiences to explore next)

1. TURKISH (Priority 1)
   - Smallest decline (-16.5%), showing relative resilience despite market-wide decline
   - Article share of edition barely changed (-0.2%), indicating the English language content holds up better than Turkish Wikipedia average
   - Significant audience (256 daily views)
   - High confidence data

2. POLISH (Priority 2)
   - Second-best performer with -18.3% decline
   - Largest absolute audience (296 daily views) = largest potential market
   - More stable than Czech and Ukrainian
   - High confidence data

3. CZECH (Priority 3)
   - Steeper decline (-22.8%)
   - Seasonal pattern offers opportunity (peaks Feb, lowest Jul) for targeted campaigns
   - Smallest audience (107 daily views)
   - High confidence data

4. UKRAINIAN (Priority 4)
   - Steepest decline (-35.6%)
   - Rapid month-over-month erosion (-3.8% per month)
   - Warrants investigation into root causes (education shifts, platform preference changes)
   - Second-largest initial audience (279 views) = high-priority for understanding decline

STRATEGIC GUIDANCE
Start market validation in Turkish and Polish markets, where English language interest appears more stable. Investigate Czech's seasonality for targeted go-to-market timing. Deep-dive into Ukrainian market dynamics to understand the sharp decline before investing resources. Validate all findings with direct user research, competitive analysis, and language learning adoption trends in each region.

IMPORTANT: Wikipedia pageviews measure attention only, not purchase intent or demand. Supplement with traditional market research before final market prioritization decisions.
